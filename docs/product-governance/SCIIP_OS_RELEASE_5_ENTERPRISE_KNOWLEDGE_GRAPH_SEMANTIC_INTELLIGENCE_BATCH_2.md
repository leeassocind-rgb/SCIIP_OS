# SCIIP_OS Release 5 — Enterprise Knowledge Graph & Semantic Intelligence Batch 2

Version 137.0.0 establishes a governed cross-property knowledge graph, semantic index, ontology registry, graph traversal and analytics, evidence lineage, provenance, inference, and a dedicated workspace.

## Governance
- Graph mutations are traceable and duplicate-safe.
- Evidence lineage and ontology governance are required.
- Consequential merge, deletion, or external publication requires broker approval.
- Autonomous consequential actions remain blocked.
