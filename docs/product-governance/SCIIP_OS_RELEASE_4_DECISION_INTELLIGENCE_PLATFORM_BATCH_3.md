# SCIIP_OS Release 4 — Decision Intelligence Platform Batch 3

## Certification target

- Application version: `93.0.0`
- Framework: `SCIIP_RELEASE_4_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_PLATFORM_BATCH_3`
- Governance tests: `52`

## Capabilities

Decision identity, scenario modeling, forecasting, risk propagation, portfolio optimization, recommendation comparison, explainability, append-only decision ledger, decision replay, sensitivity analysis, decision governance, and a unified decision runtime.

## Non-negotiable controls

- Evidence is required for recommendations.
- Explanations and confidence are mandatory.
- Consequential actions require broker approval.
- Autonomous consequential execution remains blocked.
- Decision history is append-only and duplicate safe.
