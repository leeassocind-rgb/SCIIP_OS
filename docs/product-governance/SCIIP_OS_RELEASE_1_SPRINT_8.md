# SCIIP_OS Release 1 Sprint 8

## Opportunity Intelligence and Assignment Matching

Sprint 8 combines governed assignment context, the enterprise property knowledge graph, and spatially eligible property records into an explainable opportunity-ranking workflow.

### Product outcomes

- Deterministic assignment-to-property matching
- Explicit weighted scoring for building size, geography, power, clear height, availability, evidence, and relationships
- Evidence-chain counts and confidence classifications
- Broker-controlled review and outreach approval
- Append-only, duplicate-safe opportunity decision history
- No autonomous outreach or consequential action

### Governance gates

1. Opportunity rankings must be deterministic.
2. Every score must expose component metrics, strengths, and gaps.
3. Recommendations remain advisory.
4. Broker approval is required before outreach.
5. Missing evidence lowers confidence and is never silently inferred.
6. Production build is a mandatory certification gate.
