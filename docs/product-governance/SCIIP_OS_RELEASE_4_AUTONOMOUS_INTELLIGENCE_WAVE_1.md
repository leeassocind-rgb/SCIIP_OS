# SCIIP_OS Release 4 — Autonomous Industrial Intelligence Wave 1

## Scope
This governed batch establishes the Executive Morning Brief and Decision Ledger foundations.

## Capabilities
1. Deterministic executive morning-brief generation.
2. Material market-change detection.
3. Assignment attention and priority scoring.
4. Comparable and approval aggregation.
5. Evidence snapshots frozen at recommendation time.
6. Append-only recommendation and broker-decision history.
7. Outcome capture and measured recommendation accuracy.
8. Broker-reviewed calibration guidance.
9. Duplicate-safe brief publication.
10. Governed workspace integration.

## Governance
Facts, derived intelligence, and AI-assisted narrative remain distinguishable. Missing evidence produces `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. Broker approval is required before consequential action. Autonomous outreach, commitment, pricing changes, and capability removal remain blocked. Decision and outcome records are append-only; original evidence is never overwritten.
