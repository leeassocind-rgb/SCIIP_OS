# SCIIP_OS Release 4 — Executive AI Platform Batch 4

## Governed capability
This batch establishes an evidence-grounded executive AI layer over the Property Digital Twin and Decision Intelligence platforms.

## Controls
- Broker approval is required for consequential actions.
- Autonomous consequential actions are blocked.
- External communications remain drafts until explicitly approved and sent by a broker.
- Evidence grounding and explainability are required.
- Outcome learning is append-only and advisory; it cannot overwrite broker decisions or model facts.

## Capabilities
Executive Copilot, investment memorandum generation, presentation generation, client briefs, meeting preparation, strategy composition, communication drafting, outcome learning, governed orchestration, and Executive AI Command Center.
