# SCIIP_OS Release 5.5 Sprint 1 — PDF Production Certification

## Mission
Certify the daily AIR CRE SuperSheet PDF corpus as immutable, time-series market evidence.

## Controls
- Permanent Command Center PDF drop box for daily and historical uploads.
- PDF-only intake, report-date parsing, SHA-256 identity, and exact-duplicate blocking.
- Immutable local document registry with progressive certification gates.
- Registration is separated from extraction and promotion.
- Page extraction, temporal resolution, graph projection, and AI evidence readiness remain gated.
- No source overwrite and no autonomous consequential action.
