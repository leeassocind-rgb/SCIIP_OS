# SCIIP_OS Release 5.5 Sprint 3.7

Version 196.7.0 adds review-gated identity merge governance. Every fuzzy proposal remains pending until an explicit APPROVED, REJECTED, or DEFERRED decision is appended to the immutable decision ledger. Reviewer, notes, evidence hash, timestamp, affected canonical IDs, and supersession links are preserved. Approved relationships are reversible and require temporal history recalculation before promotion. Silent merges are prohibited.
