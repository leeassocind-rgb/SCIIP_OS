# SCIIP_OS Release 1 Sprint 10

## Executive Command Center

Sprint 10 unifies governed property intelligence into one executive operating view. It aggregates assignment context, property-graph evidence, opportunity decisions, workflow health, broker approvals, notifications, and append-only timeline activity without bypassing any existing approval gate.

## Governance

- Cross-workspace context is assignment scoped.
- Executive metrics are deterministic aggregations of existing governed stores.
- Notifications are advisory and broker controlled.
- Consequential actions remain blocked until explicit broker approval.
- Timeline activity is derived from preserved ledgers and is not rewritten.
- Every executive card routes to its underlying evidence workspace.
