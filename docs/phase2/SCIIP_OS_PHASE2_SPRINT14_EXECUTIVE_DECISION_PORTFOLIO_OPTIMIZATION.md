# SCIIP_OS Phase 2 Sprint 14 — Executive Decision Portfolio Optimization

Adds a read-only, advisory-only optimizer across executive scenario candidates. The engine enumerates candidate portfolios, enforces explicit capital, capacity, risk, selection, category, and diversification constraints, ranks feasible portfolios, identifies a Pareto-style frontier, explains the winning portfolio, and preserves full source-signal lineage.

It cannot mutate workflows or execute recommendations. Every result requires human review.
