# SCIIP_OS Phase 2 Sprint 22

## Executive Governance Recommendation Review and Decision Ledger

Sprint 22 records explicit human disposition of Sprint 21 governance recommendations.

### Capabilities

- Approve, reject, or defer recommendations
- Require identified human actors and reasons
- Require evidence for approvals
- Resolve recommendation conflicts
- Append immutable decision records
- Preserve advisory, evidence, and source-signal lineage
- Block incomplete or unsupported reviews

### Governance guarantees

- Human decision required
- Append-only decision ledger
- No workflow mutation
- No autonomous execution
- No automatic recommendation dispatch
- Duplicate-safe decision recording

### Runtime capability

`executive-governance-recommendation-review-decision-ledger`

Depends on:

`executive-autonomous-governance-advisor`
