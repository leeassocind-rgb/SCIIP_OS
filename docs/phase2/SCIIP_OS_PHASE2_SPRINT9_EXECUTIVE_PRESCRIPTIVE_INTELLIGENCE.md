# SCIIP_OS Phase 2 Sprint 9 — Executive Prescriptive Intelligence

Adds an advisory-only prescriptive layer over governed predictive and effectiveness intelligence.

## Components
- Evidence normalization and strength scoring
- Constraint evaluation
- Alternative analysis
- Recommendation ranking
- Immutable recommendation scorecards
- Human-governed executive playbooks

## Governance
The layer is read-only and advisory-only. It cannot mutate workflows, approve actions, or execute recommendations. Every playbook step requires the existing human approval path. Full signal lineage is retained.
