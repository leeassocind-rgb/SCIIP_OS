# SCIIP_OS Phase 2 Sprint 8 — Executive Predictive Intelligence

Adds governed, read-only forecasting over immutable executive portfolio snapshots.

## Capabilities
- health trend forecasting
- risk trajectory forecasting
- conservative, baseline, and optimistic scenarios
- leading-indicator detection
- immutable executive forecast scorecards
- duplicate-safe bounded ingestion

## Governance
All output is advisory-only. Workflow mutation and autonomous execution remain disabled. Full decision traceability is preserved.
